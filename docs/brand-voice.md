# Spock's Resale Shop Brand Voice Guide

## Brand Personality

- **Honest:** Clear condition notes, realistic expectations, no fluff.
- **Friendly:** Conversational and welcoming, never cold or corporate.
- **Slightly witty:** Smart, light humor in small doses.
- **Adventurous:** Emphasize discovery, sourcing stories, and changing inventory.
- **Customer-focused:** Help buyers decide quickly with practical detail and transparency.

## Words We Use

- Curated
- Discovery
- Found
- Sourced
- One-of-a-kind
- Restored / cleaned / tested
- Ready for its next chapter
- Fair price
- Condition notes
- Shop with confidence

## Words We Avoid

- Hype-heavy claims (e.g., "insane deal", "must buy now")
- Overpromises (e.g., "perfect", "mint" unless objectively true)
- Slang-heavy phrasing that feels unserious
- Franchise affiliation language or references implying endorsement
- Jargon that hides product condition

## Tone Rules

1. Lead with useful product truth, then personality.
2. Keep copy concise and scannable.
3. Use "Live Long and Prosper" sparingly for memorable moments.
4. Prioritize trust language over trend language.
5. Mention flaws plainly when they exist.

## Example Product Descriptions

### Example 1 (Home)
**Walnut Floating Shelves Set**  
Found during a clearance overstock run and still in excellent shape. Includes hidden mounting hardware and clean edges. One tiny nick on the back side is not visible after installation. A practical storage upgrade with vintage warmth.

### Example 2 (Electronics)
**Sony Cassette Boombox CFD-S70**  
Sourced from a thrift electronics shelf, then fully tested in-shop. Cassette playback, CD function, and radio reception all work as expected. Top panel has light cosmetic scuffs, reflected in price.

### Example 3 (Collectible)
**Atari 2600 Joystick Pair (For Parts/Repair)**  
Original shells and cables, sold for restoration projects. One stick has intermittent directional response and both need internal cleaning. Great option for tinkerers who enjoy bringing classics back to life.

## Example Social Captions

1. **"Today on The Hunt:** pulled a vintage lamp from an estate sale study, rewired it, and listed it by sunset. Proof that good design deserves a second life."
2. "Fresh finds just landed: tested audio gear, vintage kitchen favorites, and a few oddities we couldn’t leave behind."
3. "Some items are one-and-done. If it speaks to you, don’t wait too long."

## Example Customer-Service Language

### Shipping Update
"Your order is packed and on the way. Tracking has been sent to your inbox. If anything looks off when it arrives, message us and we’ll make it right."

### Condition Clarification
"Great question. This item is listed as **Good** because it has light surface wear on the top panel, but all core functions tested successfully."

### Return Support
"Thanks for reaching out. We can help with a return if the item arrived damaged or materially different from the listing. Send photos and your order number, and we’ll take care of next steps."
